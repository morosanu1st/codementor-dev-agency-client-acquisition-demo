# Outreach Templates

Rules: short (under 120 words), one clear ask, personalized first line, follow up 2–3 times spaced 3–5 days apart. Track every send in the leads CSV.

## 1. Cold email

**Subject:** quick idea for {{company}}'s {{area: booking flow / website leads / invoice processing}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{specific observation: your booking page takes 6 steps on mobile / you're hiring 2 people for data entry}}.

We're a small dev team that helps {{ICP}} {{outcome}}. For {{similar client type}} we {{result with number, e.g. cut checkout drop-off by 30% in 5 weeks}}.

Would a 15-minute call next week be useful to see if something similar fits {{company}}? Happy to share a 3-point teardown either way.

Best,
{{your_name}} — {{agency}} · {{link to case study}}

**Follow-up 1 (day 4):** "Bumping this up — here's the 3-point teardown I mentioned: {{1–2 lines}}. Worth a quick chat?"
**Follow-up 2 (day 9):** "Last note from me — if {{pain}} isn't a priority right now, no worries. Should I check back next quarter?"

## 2. LinkedIn

**Connection request (≤300 chars):**
Hi {{first_name}} — saw {{post / company news}}. I work with {{ICP}} on {{service}}. Would be great to connect.

**After they accept:**
Thanks for connecting! Curious — how are you handling {{pain}} at {{company}} today? We recently helped a {{similar company}} {{result}}. If it's on your radar I can send a short breakdown of how we did it.

## 3. Referral ask (to happy clients and partners)

Hi {{first_name}},

Really enjoyed working on {{project}} — glad {{result}} worked out.

We're taking on 2 new projects next month. Do you know 1–2 people at {{type of company}} who are dealing with {{pain}}? A short intro email is all I'd need — I'll take it from there and keep you posted.

As a thank-you, {{optional: referral fee / free month of maintenance}}.

Thanks!
{{your_name}}

**Tip:** ask at moments of peak happiness — launch day, first positive metric, after a good review.
