Fill this in once, then revisit every quarter. A clear answer here makes every outreach message, proposal and case study easier to write.

## 2. Ideal Client Profile (ICP)
- **Industry / vertical:** e.g. clinics, logistics, B2B SaaS, e-commerce
- **Size:** e.g. 10–200 employees, has budget ≥ EUR 5k per project
- **Decision maker:** e.g. founder, COO, head of marketing, CTO
- **Trigger events:** new funding, hiring a marketing lead, outdated website, manual process slowing ops, launching a new product
- **Pains (in their words):** "our site doesn't bring leads", "we lose hours on manual data entry", "our app reviews are terrible"
- **Red flags (don't chase):** no budget owner, "just need a quick copy of X for cheap", unclear scope + fixed tiny price

## 3. Positioning statement
> We help **[ICP]** who struggle with **[pain]** get **[measurable outcome]** through **[your service]**, without **[common fear: long timelines / big agency prices / tech jargon]**.

Example: *We help 10–100 person logistics companies cut manual paperwork by 50%+ with AI document automation, delivered in 6 weeks, without replacing their current systems.*
