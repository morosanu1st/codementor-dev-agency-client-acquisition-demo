# Discovery Call Script (30 min)

Goal: decide together if there's a fit, understand the problem + budget + decision process, and agree a next step. You talk ~30%, they talk ~70%.

## 0. Before the call (5 min)
- Check their website, LinkedIn, recent news; note 2 observations.
- Have 1 relevant case study ready.

## 1. Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for making time. My plan: I'll ask a few questions about where you are and what you want to achieve, then share how we've approached similar situations, and we'll decide if a next step makes sense. Sound good?"

## 2. Situation & pain (10 min)
- What prompted you to look at this now?
- How are you handling it today? What's not working?
- What does that cost you (time, revenue, customers)?
- What have you tried already?

## 3. Desired outcome (5 min)
- If we're talking 6 months from now and this went great, what changed?
- How will you measure success? (leads/month, hours saved, conversion, app rating)

## 4. Budget, timeline, decision (5 min)
- Is there a deadline or event driving the timeline?
- Projects like this typically land between **EUR X and Y** depending on scope — is that in the range you expected?
- Who else is involved in the decision? What does your approval process look like?

## 5. Fit + next step (5 min)
- Recap: "So you need **[outcome]** by **[date]**, main constraint is **[x]**. Did I get that right?"
- Share 1 relevant case study (2 minutes max).
- Next step: "I'll send a 1-page proposal with 2 options by **[day]**. Can we book 20 minutes on **[day]** to walk through it?" → book it before hanging up.

## Disqualify politely if
No budget owner, no real pain, or expectations far from your range: "Based on what you've shared, I don't think we're the best fit right now — here's what I'd suggest instead…"
