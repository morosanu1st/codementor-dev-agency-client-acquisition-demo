# 4-Week Mentoring Plan: Repeatable Client Acquisition for a Small Dev Agency

Format: 1 x 60-min session per week + async review of your materials (messages, proposals, CSV pipeline) between sessions. Each week ends with a concrete deliverable.

## Week 1 — Positioning & offer
- Analyze past projects → pick 1 primary niche (and 1 backup) using `01-ideal-client-profile.md`.
- Write the positioning statement and the offer ladder (entry audit → core project → retainer).
- Set the revenue goal and baseline numbers; run `python3 pipeline.py` with your own goal/rates.
- **Deliverable:** 1-page positioning doc + priced offer ladder + first pipeline report.

## Week 2 — Portfolio & case studies
- Turn 2–3 past projects into case studies: problem → approach → result (with numbers).
- Rework the website/landing page around the niche (hero line, proof, one call-to-action).
- If proof is thin for the niche: build 1 small demo project that shows the exact service.
- Polish Clutch / Upwork / LinkedIn profiles to match the same positioning.
- **Deliverable:** 2+ case studies live, updated homepage hero + profiles.

## Week 3 — Outbound system & channels
- Build a list of 100 target accounts that match the ICP (LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Apollo / Clutch directories).
- Channel mix and weekly cadence, sized by the pipeline tool's weekly targets:
  - **LinkedIn:** connection + value message, 2–3 posts/week about niche problems.
  - **Cold email:** 3-step sequence from `02-outreach-templates.md`, warmed-up domain, plain text.
  - **Upwork:** 5–10 tailored proposals/week to niche jobs, profile + specialized profile.
  - **Clutch / directories:** collect 3+ reviews, respond to inbound quickly.
  - **Referrals & partners:** referral ask to every happy client; partner with designers/marketing agencies.
- Set up the lead CSV (or CRM) and a weekly 30-min pipeline review; follow up on every stale lead flagged.
- **Deliverable:** outbound running at target volume for 5 days, all leads tracked.

## Week 4 — Sales calls, pricing & retention
- Role-play discovery calls with `03-discovery-call-script.md`; review a real call recording if available.
- Pricing: move from hourly to value/phase-based pricing with 3 options (`04-proposal-template.md`).
- Proposal walk-through practice + objection handling (price, timing, "we'll think about it").
- Retention: maintenance/iteration retainer, quarterly business review, referral moment after launch.
- **Deliverable:** proposal template finalized, retainer offer defined, 30/60/90-day acquisition plan.

## After week 4
Monthly check-in: review conversion rates per stage, fix the weakest stage first (low reply rate → messaging/targeting; low call→proposal → qualification; low proposal→won → pricing/proof).
